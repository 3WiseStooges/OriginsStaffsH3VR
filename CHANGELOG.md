# Changelog

## 0.1.2

- The trigger hand holds the staff 2 in higher: the pointer finger now rests at the top of the
  blue rings instead of their base.

## 0.1.1

- The fire staffs' three fireballs leave together from the tip again; the side two were coming
  out too far ahead of the staff.
- The mod's icon is now the Origins Staffs folder thumbnail.

## 0.1.0

- Playable Staff of Fire and Kagutsuchi's Blood, built from BO2's own first-person
  models, sounds and reload animation.
- Two-handed hold: black handle (grip only) and blue rings (trigger, release, reload lever).
- Pump-action crystal reload behind a release button.
- Both fire staffs throw three fireballs per pull, fanned 15 degrees apart, as in BO2. Base staff:
  9 charges. Upgraded: 12 charges, and outward knives that cut and stab like H3VR's knives.
- Spawner folder Firearms > Exotic > Origins Staffs with its own thumbnail.
- The full set: Staff of Ice / Ullr's Arrow (6 / 9 charges), Staff of Lightning / Kimat's Bite
  (12 / 18) and Staff of Wind / Boreas' Fury (3 / 6), each with its own tip, crystal, reload,
  sounds and effects from BO2. Their shots follow BO2's own weapon files and staff scripts:
  - Ice: a spray of icicles; up to three sosigs within 15 m and 25 degrees freeze with an icicle
    in the chest, then shatter (or take the hit and thaw, if too tough). Ullr's Arrow throws
    twelve icicles and freezes up to five.
  - Lightning: slow balls that burst over 2.4 m (upgraded 3.3 m), killing or stunning.
  - Wind: a fast ball of wind that blows away every sosig within 2 m of it, killing and flinging
    them, or knocking them down. Boreas' Fury also sweeps BO2's cone: up to 12 sosigs within 10 m
    and 60 degrees.
- Ice and wind projectiles also hit any sosig they touch directly.
- Every upgraded staff's tip blades cut and stab.
- Upgraded staffs charge like BO2's: hold the trigger and let go for a level 2 or 3 charged shot
  (fire grenades and burning pools, a blizzard, a draining lightning ball, a whirlwind).
- Upgraded staffs have their own spawner icons, outlined in red as BO2's HUD shows them.
