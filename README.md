# Origins Staffs

The four elemental staffs from Call of Duty: Black Ops II Zombies' **Origins** map, brought to
H3VR — each in its base and upgraded form.

> **Work in progress.** Nothing is playable yet; this page describes the planned mod.

## The staffs

| Staff | Element | Base shots per crystal | Upgraded shots per crystal |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kagutsuchi | Fire | 9 | 12 |
| Ull | Ice | 6 | 9 |
| Kimat | Lightning | 12 | 18 |
| Boreas | Wind | 3 | 6 |

Shot counts come straight from the game's weapon files.

## Reloading

No magic ammo counter: each staff is reloaded by hand like any other H3VR gun. Pull the spent
crystal from the socket near your grip and push in a fresh one of the matching element. A fire
crystal will not fit the ice staff.
