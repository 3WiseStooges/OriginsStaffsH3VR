# Origins Staffs

The four elemental staffs from Call of Duty: Black Ops II Zombies' **Origins** map, brought to
H3VR, each in its base and upgraded form. Find them in the item spawner under
**Firearms > Exotic > Origins Staffs**.

## The staffs

| Staff | Upgraded | Shot | Charges (base / upgraded) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Staff of Fire | Kagutsuchi's Blood | Three fireballs in a fan that burn and set sosigs alight. | 9 / 12 |
| Staff of Ice | Ullr's Arrow | A spray of icicles. Up to three sosigs in front of you freeze, then shatter; upgraded, twelve icicles freeze up to five. | 6 / 9 |
| Staff of Lightning | Kimat's Bite | Rapid, slow lightning balls that burst and shock everything nearby. | 12 / 18 |
| Staff of Wind | Boreas' Fury | A fast ball of wind that blows away every sosig within 2 m of it. Upgraded, it also sweeps a wide cone in front. | 3 / 6 |

Charges, fire rate and projectile speed come straight from the game's weapon files; for ice,
lightning and wind, so do range, cone width and damage (from the staff scripts).
Each upgraded staff also flips blades out of its tip that cut and stab like H3VR's knives.

## Holding and reloading

- Hold the staff by the blue rings: trigger, plus the release button (A/X in Streamlined
  controls, touchpad up in Classic).
- Steady it with your other hand anywhere on the lower half.
- Upgraded staffs charge, as in BO2: tap the trigger for a normal shot, or hold it and let go to
  fire a charged shot (level 2 after 1 s, level 3 after 2 s; one charge per level). Fire throws
  grenades that leave burning pools, ice leaves a blizzard, lightning sends out a slow ball that
  drains everything near it, and wind leaves a whirlwind that drags sosigs in.
- To reload, press release, then pump. Pull the hands together to draw the crystal out, then
  push them apart to seat it again.
